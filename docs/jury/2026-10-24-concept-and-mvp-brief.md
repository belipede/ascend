# Product brief for jury review (Phase 0)

## Concept
Ascend is a native iPhone app that links long-term goals to daily behaviour: **GOAL → PLAN → HABIT → TASK → PROGRESS**. Example: yearly goal "Read 24 books" → monthly milestone "2 books" → daily habit "read 20 min" → today's task "read 10 pages, log progress". The pitch: help people see why today's actions matter, without guilt or a complicated system.

## Situation
- Solo founder (Ben), building with Claude Code. Immediate goal: a reliable app on Ben's own iPhone. Longer-term: possibly App Store and monetisation.
- No users, no research, no revenue, no budget committed. Development requires a Mac (cloud sessions are Linux).
- Local-only data, no backend, no third-party dependencies for MVP.

## Ben's full requirement list (from the master prompt)
Habits (schedules, quantities, notes, pause/rest, streaks, calendars, 7/30/365 rates), tasks (deadlines, priorities, recurrence, subtasks, search/filter, rescheduling, goal links), goals (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly, milestones, progress bars, linked habits/tasks, no double counting, archive), reminders (scheduled, morning plan, evening review, quiet hours, encouraging messages), daily dashboard, calendar and daily/weekly reviews, statistics, Home and Lock Screen widgets with interactive actions, polished accessible design.

## Proposed MVP (for the jury to challenge)
Phase 1 foundation, then Phases 2–5 for a personal MVP:
- **In**: habits (daily, selected weekdays, N× per week; check-off or quantity), streaks and 7/30-day rates, pause; tasks (due date, priority, one-level subtasks, simple recurrence, overdue carries forward); goals (target + deadline, milestones, link habits/tasks, single-source progress); Today dashboard; per-item local reminders; one Home Screen widget (Today) with interactive check-off; JSON export.
- **Deferred**: Lock Screen widgets, morning/evening review notifications, weekly/monthly calendar views, statistics screen beyond basic rates, search/filter beyond a simple search, categories/icons library, encouraging message engine, quiet hours (OS Focus covers much of it), any cloud/AI/subscription.

## Open questions for the jury
1. Is the connected goal → habit → task loop a real differentiator, or a feature list competitors already cover?
2. Is the proposed MVP the smallest version that proves the idea? What should be cut or added?
3. Who is the primary user?
4. What must be validated before Phase 6, and how cheaply?
5. Key legal, cost, operational and design risks for a local-only personal MVP, and what changes at public launch.
